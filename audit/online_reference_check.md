# Online reference check

Checked: 27 July 2026

## Legal and institutional status

- EU-Mercosur negotiations on the trade component concluded on 29 June 2019.
- The parties reached a political agreement on the improved Partnership Agreement on 6 December 2024.
- The EU and Mercosur signed the Partnership Agreement and interim Trade Agreement on 17 January 2026.
- The interim Trade Agreement began provisional application on 1 May 2026.
- Source: [European Commission, EU-Mercosur text of the agreement](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/eu-trade-relationships-country-and-region/countries-and-regions/mercosur/eu-mercosur-agreement/text-agreement_en).

- The EU-Colombia/Ecuador/Peru agreement has applied to Peru and Colombia since 2013 and Ecuador since 2017.
- The Commission lists the October 2023 staff report and the final ex post evaluation volumes on its evaluation index.
- The final evaluation report is also registered by the Publications Office with DOI `10.2781/92363`.
- Sources: [Commission ex post evaluations](https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/analysis-and-assessment/ex-post-evaluations_en) and [Publications Office record](https://op.europa.eu/es/publication-detail/-/publication/f09dba9e-aa5a-11ec-83e1-01aa75ed71a1).

- Current EUDR application dates are 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and for micro and small operators already covered by the EU Timber Regulation, and 30 June 2027 for other micro and small operators.
- The Commission adopted further product-scope and information-system measures on 13 July 2026.
- Sources: [Commission EUDR overview](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/forests/deforestation/regulation-deforestation-free-products_en) and [13 July 2026 implementation update](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-updates-product-scope-and-tools-support-eudr-2026-07-13_en).

## Evidence limits retained in the chapter

- The 2020 Mercosur Sustainability Impact Assessment is treated as scenario-based evidence, not an observed environmental outcome.
- The Andean evaluation is treated as an institutional assessment, not a causal test proving that the agreement produced specific environmental effects.
- The chapter does not claim that the EU-Mercosur agreements or the EUDR have already caused a measured reduction in deforestation.
