# Citation audit resolution

Applied to `draft/GreenIntegration_RHandbook_V2.docx` on 30 July 2026.

## Corrections applied

- Removed the unsupported claim that the relevant agreement texts are approximately
  80% similar and removed its misattribution to Cesar de Oliveira et al. (2024).
- Replaced it with a narrower analytical statement that does not make an unsupported
  quantitative claim.
- Added the complete reference for Lavenex (2004).
- Retained Bradford (2012) and added its complete reference. This is the original
  *Northwestern University Law Review* article and is distinct from Bradford's 2020
  book.
- Removed the ambiguous `IRIS (2020)` citation. The identifiable IRIS/Bocconi record
  is a 2018 publication edited by Antonella Mori, not a 2020 source. Damro (2015)
  remains attached to the relevant sentence.
- Assigned `2026a` to the European Commission agreement-text page, `2026b` to the
  EUDR overview, `2026c` to the EU-Mercosur TSD factsheet and `2026d` to the July
  EUDR implementation update. The reference list now follows this sequence.
- Removed the duplicate standalone entry for Regulation (EU) 2023/1115.
- Added the Bradford (2012) and Lavenex (2004) records to `references.bib`.

## Recommendations not applied verbatim

- The audit recommended changing Bradford (2012) to Bradford (2020). Online
  verification showed that both works exist and that the 2012 article directly
  supports the claim about external regulatory reach. Treating the date as an error
  would have conflated two distinct publications.
- The audit recommended adding `IRIS (2020)`. No matching 2020 bibliographic record
  was established. The identifiable publication is *EU and Latin America: A Stronger
  Partnership?* (Mori, 2018), so the ambiguous citation was removed rather than
  reconstructed speculatively.
